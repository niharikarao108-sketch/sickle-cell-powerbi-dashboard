# Sickle Cell Disease Burden & Program Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize and understand the burden of Sickle Cell Disease (SCD) in Chhattisgarh.

The information was taken from existing NSCAEM reports and presentations. The project focused on converting the available information into clear and interactive visualizations to make important trends and patterns easier to understand.

## Objectives

* Present Sickle Cell Disease information in an easy-to-understand visual format
* Visualize screening, disease and trait burden, treatment uptake, and age-wise patterns
* Identify important gaps and areas that may require attention
* Present healthcare insights through an interactive dashboard

## Dashboard Features

* Screening coverage
* Disease and trait distribution
* Age-wise disease and trait burden
* Gender-wise distribution
* District-level burden
* Treatment uptake
* AB-PMJAY-related information
* Sickle Cell screening patterns

## Key Observations

* Differences in disease and trait burden across age groups
* Variation in screening patterns across age groups
* Low treatment uptake compared with the identified disease burden
* Differences in Sickle Cell burden across districts
* Gaps in treatment access and continuity of care

## Recommendations

Based on the information visualized in the dashboard, the project highlights potential areas for improvement:

* Strengthen last-mile diagnostic services
* Improve access to treatment at the district level
* Strengthen patient follow-up and digital tracking
* Improve community awareness and outreach
* Strengthen availability of medicines and diagnostic resources

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Data Visualization
* Healthcare Analytics

## Data Source

The dashboard was developed using information from the National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission (NSCAEM) and related reports and presentations.

The information represented in the dashboard was based on data available as of **October 2024**.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent healthcare data visualization project. The underlying information was obtained from existing reports and presentations. The project does not represent primary screening, patient-level data collection, or clinical data collection conducted by the author.
